Student Fee & Attendance
Management System

"An All-in-One Sports Academy Management Software for Student Management, Attendance Tracking, Fee Collection, Performance Monitoring, and Parent Communication."

Secure Admin Access

The system includes password-protected administrator access to ensure data security and privacy. Only authorized staff members can access sensitive student information, attendance records, and financial data.

Dashboard Overview

The Dashboard provides a complete overview of academy operations in one place. Administrators can instantly view active students, pending fees, upcoming payments, and recently joined students. This centralized dashboard helps academy management track important activities and make informed decisions quickly.

Student & Batch Management

The Student Management module allows administrators to efficiently manage all student records. It includes student profile creation, batch allocation, parent contact information, enrollment details, and status tracking. Advanced search and filtering options make it easy to find and manage students within seconds.

The Batch Management feature allows academies to organize students into different training groups based on skill level, age, or schedule. Batch-wise attendance, fee management, and performance tracking help streamline academy operations.

Attendance & Player Performance

The Attendance Management system simplifies daily attendance tracking for multiple batches and sessions. Coaches can mark students as Present, Absent, or Off for each session while also managing holidays and batch schedules. This ensures accurate attendance records and better student monitoring.

The Player Performance section helps coaches monitor the growth and development of students. Performance records, achievements, skill assessments, and training progress can be maintained systematically, enabling personalized coaching and better athlete development.

Fee Management

The Fee Management Ledger provides complete control over student fee collections. Administrators can track monthly payments, identify overdue fees, view payment history, and manage batch-wise fee structures. The system helps maintain transparent and organized financial records.

Pending Payments & Reminders

The Pending Payments module automatically identifies students with outstanding fees and provides quick access to parent contact details.

Integrated WhatsApp communication enables quick and professional communication with parents. With a single click, administrators can send payment reminders, important announcements, schedule updates, and notifications instantly, improving engagement and reducing communication gaps.

Key Features

  • Monthly Attendance PDF Download
  • Fee Payment WhatsApp Reminder
  • Real-time Attendance & Fee Tracking
  • Student & Batch Management
  • Detailed Reports & Analytics
  • Unlimited Students & Lifetime Access
